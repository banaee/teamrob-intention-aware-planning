Ruled by Hadi on 6 October 2026 (design_records.md, "T-K", STEP 6). This is debugging, not the evaluation. The set
covers various situations roughly. Nothing is adjusted to a result, and a case that does not occur is recorded as
absent. The form is kitting's: step 5e's authoring and T-F part 1's measurement.

Data and figures are not in git (analysis/README.md). The runs are under `measurement/<scenario>/<run>/`, the result
table is `measurement/results.csv`, the tags are `measurement/tags.csv`, and the comparison report is `COMPARISON.md`
(with `comparison.html`), generated by `comparison.py`.

## The two points confirmed before the authoring (6 October 2026)

Both were checked in-process, on the scenarios as they stood before this step.

1. **Is the recognizer's output identical with an idle and a working robot?** For a script independent of the robot,
   yes. All 20 kind-3 scenarios (scenario_s08_01 to _10, s09_01 to _10) were run with context knowledge on, once as
   written and once with the robot's pool emptied. Every `[IR`-prefixed log line was identical over 900 ticks.
   - The new scripts therefore have no idle form; their recognition measures are read from the working runs.
   - The recognition set (scenario_s02_02 to _19, s04_02 to _19, s06_02 to _19) is run in the two intention-aware
     conditions only.
2. **Does a human-unaware robot move as the robot alone in a script that depends on the robot?** Yes, in all twelve
   such scripts (scenario_s03_02, _03, s05_02 to _06, s07_02 to _06). The robot's position and microaction were
   identical on every tick of 1000. No robot task of stage 1 reads a fact the human's tasks change (the scan's
   is_scanned).
   - The reference check therefore applies to these scripts too (`analysis/instruments/mpb/reference_check.py`).
   - The objects are still not separate by G's rule, so the oracle stays off for them.

## The timelines (written before the runs, never moved)

Each planning script has two or three copies. A copy differs from its script only in where break_time lies. No copy
holds a window over the whole run. The windows are placed by `make_set.py`'s `windows()`.

For a script independent of the robot, the windows come from the base's replay, from the stretches of the task on top
of the human's stack:

- **V1:** over the first break's stretch. A coffee break makes the fact in accord with the task; an office break makes
  it not in accord. A script with no break gets V1 over its first scan instead.
- **V2:** over the first stretch that does not overlap V1. Scans are taken first, then any other task: a stand, or the
  walk to the desk.
- **V3:** for a script with two breaks, over both.
- **Script f:** in place of V1 to V3, KT4's three edges. The window opens 10 ticks before the human leaves for the
  coffee break, at the middle of its walk, or at its arrival. Each window ends with the coffee break's stretch.

For a script that depends on the robot, the windows come from the robot's plain chain (horizon.py). V1 is
[first delivery's completion, + 60). V2 is [second delivery's completion, + 60), or [0, 60) when there is one
delivery. The run shows what each task met.

The recognition set gets no copies. Its scripts are those of the planning set, and their recognizer output with a
working robot is the same (point 1).

## The conditions and the runs

The run files are `configs/dock_loading/tk6/<scenario>/run_NNN.yaml`, one per run. A run is named by its serial; no
setting appears in any name. The settings are columns of the result table. Assignment knowledge is on in every run; the strategy is
single_task, and also full_reorder for the planning scripts (run_569 to run_864); every comparison of conditions stays
inside one strategy.

| part | count |
|---|---|
| 74 planning scripts in four conditions (human-unaware, intention-unaware, intention-aware with context knowledge off, with it on) | run_001 to run_296 |
| 164 copies with break_time, intention-aware with context knowledge on | run_297 to run_460 |
| 54 recognition scenarios, intention-aware with context knowledge off and on | run_461 to run_568 |
| the 74 planning scripts under full_reorder in the four conditions (Hadi, 6 October 2026; the step's second ruling) | run_569 to run_864 |

The expectations come before the runs. For every run of a script independent of the robot (478; the scripts that
depend on it, among them the recognition set's C13, C14 and M4, have none, MPB-DL3), the human's replay and the oracle's
per-tick table were written at the run's step cap (`expect.sh`). Their md5s are in `expectations_md5.md`, committed
before any run. Each run's own `trajectory.json` and `expected_ticks.json` are checked against them after the runs.

The runner is `analysis/instruments/mpb/run_set.sh dock_loading -o analysis/dock_loading/tk6/measurement <run files>`.
It runs in copies of the committed tree, in parallel. After it come `table.py` (results.csv),
`analysis/instruments/mpb/tag.py` (tags.csv) and `comparison.py` (COMPARISON.md).

## The tag per task (`analysis/instruments/mpb/tag.py`)

Each task the human performs is tagged by the world's facts at its first tick:

- the timeline's windows;
- the recency facts of the foreseeable tasks the human completed.

The task a fact makes more likely is taken from the declared context knowledge, as the foreseeable tasks at the raised
level. "Admitted" is reported in both readings: the gate's answer per tick, and the meta-planner's decision record.

**Provisional, for Hadi's confirmation (TODO-185):** a fact that lowers a task gives no tag. The `lowered` column
lists the tasks at the suppressed level instead.
