# T-K part 1, step 5b: kitting's existing sets with context knowledge on: the report

Stage 3 (4 October 2026). One report for both sets: the recognition set (`analysis/kitting/irb/tk5b/`, 17 scenarios,
the robot idle) and the planning set (this folder, 16 scenarios, the robot working). The sets, their expectations and
md5s: the two READMEs. Off is each set's reference (context knowledge off); on is the same run file with
`context_knowledge: true`. Assignment knowledge on, single_task, θ = 0.75, test level 0.05.

**The state this step meets.** No setup or scenario of these sets states a timeline, so with context knowledge on only
the state with no raising fact occurs. The rooms hold no A/C switch. coffee_break, the one foreseeable task, has the
ordinary strength 0.02, or the suppressed 0.005 for the 90 ticks after an observed coffee break. The prior is therefore
0.49 for each of two live deliveries, 0.98 for a lone live delivery, and 1 for coffee_break when no delivery is live
(as off). env_layout_13 (scenario_s10_11) has no coffee machine, so there the prior equals off's. A basic check, not a
coverage set.

## 1. The chain works

All 33 runs with context knowledge on agree with the oracle: 0 disagreements at 1e-9 in the 17 recognition runs, and 0
per-tick, decision and log disagreements in the 16 planning runs. The runs left the expectations' md5s unchanged. The
control (scenario_s10_06) equals its robot-alone reference in completion and in every robot position (P8a to P8c).
scenario_s10_11, the room with no foreseeable task, gives a log identical to off's except the `[run]` field and the
`[IR-context]` lines, with an identical `.rec`. Before the step, both references were rerun at HEAD into another
folder and were byte-identical (logs, `.rec`, every instrument output). No framework file changed in this step.

## 2. What the prior does to the human's deviations (the recognition set)

The prior moves admission toward the assigned deliveries and away from the coffee break. Every delivery is admitted
earlier or at the same tick:
- the first delivery of two at 8 ticks against 25;
- a lone delivery on its stretch's first tick (admitted on the previous task's pin tick) against 5 to 25 ticks.

The coffee break is admitted 18 to 30 ticks into its stretch against 10 to 12. The cost is in the deviations. Where
the human takes a coffee break between the two deliveries (s08_02, s09_02, s09_11), the lone remaining delivery is
admitted while the human walks to the machine: 22, 22 and 18 ticks, by the gate and by the trigger rule alike. Off has
no wrong admission there.

Where the human leaves a delivery, the interrupted delivery is kept longer by the gate:
- a break at the grasp: 11 ticks against 5;
- a break at the first walk: 8 against 2;
- the corner walk: 16 against 11.

The trigger rule holds it about as long as off's trigger rule did (11 against 9, 10 against 7, 16 against 16), because
off's record was already kept past the gate's dip. The change of mind (s09_07), the long stand (s09_06) and the
mid-carry break (s09_13) are unchanged. Two deviations leave a delivery that is the only assigned one live, and the
prior makes it the leader:
- the misdelivery (s09_08): item_1 is never placed on its table, stays live, and is admitted for 17 ticks on the exit
  walk;
- the delivery outside the support (s09_09): item_2 is admitted for 11 ticks while the human delivers item_3.

With context knowledge on, the trigger rule fires on the tick the gate stops clearing in every wrong admission but
one: in s08_04 and s09_04 the record bridges the gate's one-tick refusal at 30 (no observation).

## What surprised

- **scenario_s11_03.** The robot delivers next to a standing human. The prior decides between two hypotheses that the
  evidence cannot tell apart while the human stands, because both predict a walk: their tails are equal on every tick
  to 15. With context knowledge off the equal prior leaves them at 0.498 and the robot rests on the stand fallback,
  which is X1's occupied-target response. With it on, the assignment's 0.98 admits the delivery the human never makes,
  and the robot gets no hold. It is the ruled consequence (KT11's RULED line: a lone assigned task admitted on its
  commitment warrant from its prior, a retraction if the human does otherwise; AM66: a deviation is caught late). Here
  the deviation is a stand. It is caught after 16 standing ticks, two ticks after the release.
- **scenario_s10_08 reaches the cause boundary in a room with a coffee machine.** coverage.md's derivation of A4 rests
  on the tie at a reset, where coffee_break wins by its order. With the prior the deliveries hold 0.49 each at the
  reset, so the recorded delivery still leads.
- **Three cells have no instance in the set with context knowledge on, now the default.** E6 (s10_10), D9 (s11_03) and
  A8 (s10_06) each had their only instance in one scenario. P6.1, P10.10 and P11.3a to c were declared with context
  knowledge off.
- **The trigger rule and the gate agree more often with the prior.** Off, the record outlived the gate's answer in 7
  rows, by 3 to 6 ticks (s08_03, s08_04, s09_03, s09_04, s09_05). On, it did in 2 (s08_04 and s09_04 at 30: 10 ticks
  against the gate's 1). The prior keeps an interrupted delivery above θ, so the dips below θ that off's record
  bridged do not occur.
- **The misdelivered item becomes the lone assigned task.** It is admitted on commitment after the other delivery
  (17 ticks on the IRB's exit walk; 3 ticks before the planning run's terminal decision). Off, coffee_break took that
  place.
- **A retraction reported with the gate below θ** (s10_04, s12_02 at 84). The cause is the recorded delivery's
  inadequacy; the gate asks θ before adequacy, so it reports below_theta. This is as built (C3; AD1), noted only
  because it reads oddly in the log.

- One strategy (single_task). Every coverage instance of the set is claimed under it.
