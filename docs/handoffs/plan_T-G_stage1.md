## 3. The mechanisms outside the domain

### 3a. A3, the human's script form (`shared/types.py`, `world/`)

Form:
- `Script(entries, closing=(), dependence=ScriptDependence.INDEPENDENT)`. `entries` is the priority list as written:
  ordinary entries (`TaskInstance` or `ScriptEntry`, as today) and `RepeatableEntry(task)`, a frozen type with no events
  field and no `.at` / `.during` (an event on a repeatable entry cannot be written). The constructor refuses a repeatable
  entry above an ordinary entry, and a repeatable entry in `closing`.
- Stored split: `Script.entries` (the ordinary entries, type unchanged, so every present reader is unchanged),
  `Script.repeatable`, `Script.closing`. `Script.tasks()` adds the repeatable and closing tasks after today's list.
- `ScriptDependence` (enum): `INDEPENDENT` (default), `ON_ROBOT`. The declaration concerns load-time validation and the
  run-end statement only.
Machine (`world/human_executor.py`):
- Each frame knows its entry (ordinary, repeatable, closing, or none for a `Start`'s task). An ordinary or closing entry
  is closed when its frame leaves with COMPLETED, ABANDONED or INFEASIBLE (`_pop`); SUSPENDED keeps it open; a repeatable
  entry is never closed.
- `next()` on an empty stack: (1) the priority list not finished: the applicable open ordinary entries, in order, go to
  `choose()`, the one isolated choice point (default: the first), and the chosen one is Entered; none applicable: the
  first applicable repeatable entry whose task is not complete in the present world (the skip rule,
  `planner.is_complete`); else `Wait`. (2) Finished (every ordinary entry closed): the next closing entry in written
  order if applicable, else `Wait`. (3) After the closing part: `Idle`.
- Applicability: `AdaptivePlanner.is_applicable(task, agent_id, world)`, true when decomposition raises no
  `DecompositionError`; the one definition, which A4 reads too.
- `Wait` (new `Next`): the driver stands, as for `Idle`; the record shows the empty stack as today.
Load-time replay (`check_script`):
- The machine is built without the repeatable entries (R1). The replay ends on `Idle` or `Wait`.
- Every ordinary entry closed: the machine has run the closing part. Entries left open: independent script, a
  `ScriptError` naming them as left open; dependent script, one `StillOpen` transition per ordinary entry left open and
  per closing entry not taken, in the replay's record, and the loader prints `[replay] <human> not replayed: entry=i
  <task> ... closing=j <task>` and loads. `Unfired`, `Refused` and INFEASIBLE stay load errors for both kinds.
- A walk ends where the body stops (3d; answer 5).
Run end (dependent scripts only): a hook after the headless loop writes one `StillOpen` per open entry (ordinary, open
or on the stack; closing entries not taken) into the record, a `[human] ... open:<task>` line in the run log and a
`[rec] end step=n open=<task keys>` line in the stream. Independent scripts write nothing.
Coverage: `[coverage] ... repeatable=i` and `closing=j` beside `entry=i`. `world/composition.py` reads the exit walk on
`closing[-1]` when the closing part is non-empty, else on `entries[-1]` (kitting).
Files: `shared/types.py`, `shared/planner.py`, `world/human_executor.py`, `world/record.py`, `world/composition.py`,
`mesa_sim/sim_agents.py`, `mesa_sim/sim_model.py`, `mesa_sim/run_mesa.py`. Readers: `world/queries.py`,
`mesa_sim/list_scenarios.py`, `analysis/ir_testbed/trajectory.py` and the MPB oracle (through the replay's record).
Acceptance: the maintained sets byte-identical; the ten kitting scenarios with a drop event (scenario_s01_03, s01_07,
s02_03, s03_04, s03_07, s04_02, s04_03, s05_03, s06_05, s07_02) and both test-bed sets (IR, 17 run files; MPB, 16) run
at HEAD (step 0) and after the change: the `.rec` streams and every grep of CLAUDE.md's list byte-identical. A difference
stops the step and returns to the design chat as a finding. Expected: no difference, since every kitting task has a
method with no guard, so every kitting entry is applicable whenever selected.
Tests (`tests/test_tg_script.py`): the placement refusal; priority with an entry skipped and taken later; an ordinary
entry taken once (completed, dropped, infeasible on resumption); SUSPENDED keeps it open; the skip rule and the entry
taken again after the human has left the place; `Wait`; the closing part in order; a non-applicable closing entry waits;
`Idle` after the closing part, no repeatable entry; the replay for both kinds; repeatable entries not replayed; the
run-end statement for a dependent script only; the exit walk on the last closing entry; `choose()` replaceable.

## 4. The domain's content (step 7)

The rule on methods (answer 6): a task has a method for every area its agent can be in, not for every area. The robot
can be on the truck side and in the hall; the human in the hall and in the office. An agent in another area is a defect:
no method covers it, and the absence of a method is the check (no mechanism is added).

Robot tasks (`WorkTask`s, B1). G = `move_to dock_gate`; every method guarded `in_area(?agent, <area>)`, and
`is_open(dock_gate)` where its route crosses the gate. Methods in this order (the planner takes the first applicable):
held (`holding(?agent, ?pallet)`); return-empty (`holding(?agent, ?other)`, `not_equal(?other, ?pallet)`,
`is_empty(?other)`); return-full (the same without `is_empty`, after return-empty, so it applies to a full held pallet);
default (no holding guard). The return goes to `?other_home <- home_container_of(?other)` (the origin; stage 1: the
setup's initial container) and places the pallet there.
- `deliver_pallet(?pallet, ?delivery_bay <- destination_of(?pallet))`; no condition on the pallet being full (answer 1).

## 5. The points settled

- A robot task with no applicable method: `DecompositionError` out of `MetaPlanner.update()` (through `_is_complete`),
  caught by nothing; the run stops. Stage 1 cannot reach it. Stage 2 (the robot at a closed gate) needs a ruling first
  (TODO-152; the citation of TODO-30 is corrected).
- The live set holding foreseeable tasks only (the start of every kind-2 run; between deliveries): with the prior on,
  H = {coffee_break, office_break}; the human waits or walks to the standby place; both hypotheses expect a walk
  elsewhere, become inadequate, the finding is unexplained, the gate refuses, the robot realizes against the fallback
  (P4), as for kitting's exit walk. The live set empty: not reached with the prior on while the human is in an area (at
  most one break hypothesis is retired at a time); otherwise exhausted, confidence 0, below theta, the fallback.
- A pallet's origin is the setup's initial container in stage 1 (`home_container_of`); stage 2 needs the origin
  recorded at the pick-up.
- The smallest method set: section 4. B11's optional sub-task is not used: a sub-task schema must be in the robot's task
  model, and the hypothesis space is built from every schema there, so it would become a hypothesis about the human.
- The exit walk is read on the last closing entry. The standby walk has no hypothesis in the robot's task model: a
  scenario with a standby entry is classed as containing unmodelled behaviour, and its purpose says so.
- The boundary rule and the area after "move to the gate": 3d.
- The scanned state of an empty pallet does not hold (the form's default); nothing reads it with the prior on.
- The scan and the next delivery to the same bay: covered by X1 and realization. The robot holds outside the minimum
  separation while the human scans; after the boundary the human, with nothing applicable (its next scan waits for this
  very pallet), takes the standby entry and leaves; the robot places. Measured at the milestone.
